Customer Support Operations Review - August 2026

Executive Summary

This review covers customer support operations for the month of August. Average
first-response time improved to 4.2 hours, down from 6.8 hours in July, following
the rollout of an updated ticket triage system. Customer satisfaction scores rose
to 4.3 out of 5, the highest recorded in the past twelve months. Ticket volume
increased by 9 percent month-over-month, largely due to a product update that
introduced a temporary billing display bug, now resolved.

Staffing and Training

Two new support agents completed onboarding this month, bringing the total team
size to fourteen. A refreshed training module on de-escalation techniques was
introduced, and early feedback suggests it has reduced the average handling time
for complex tickets by roughly 15 percent. Agent attrition remained low, with no
departures reported during the review period.

Channel Performance

Live chat continues to be the fastest channel, with a median resolution time of
under twenty minutes. Email support lagged behind, with a median resolution time
of just under one business day. Phone support saw the largest improvement, with
average wait times dropping from eleven minutes to under five minutes after two
additional agents were cross-trained to handle phone queues during peak hours.

Recommendations

The team recommends continuing investment in the triage system, expanding the
de-escalation training to all existing agents by the end of next quarter, and
evaluating a dedicated billing support queue given the disproportionate share of
billing-related tickets observed this month.
